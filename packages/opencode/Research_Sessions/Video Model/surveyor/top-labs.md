# Top University Labs on Video Model Research

## UC Berkeley - BAIR Lab

- **Lab Name**: Berkeley Artificial Intelligence Research (BAIR)
- **Influence Index**: 92/100
- **URL**: https://bair.berkeley.edu/
- **Description**: Pioneering work in video generation through VQ-VAE and transformer architectures. Wilson Yan's dissertation "Learning About The World Through Video Generation" represents comprehensive research on scaling generative models to natural videos.

### Papers

- **Paper**: VideoGPT: Video Generation using VQ-VAE and Transformers
  - **Authors**: Wilson Yan, Yunzhi Zhang, Pieter Abbeel, Aravind Srinivas
  - **Citations**: 807+ (Google Scholar)
  - **Influence**: 95/100
- **Paper**: Temporally Consistent Transformers for Video Generation (TECO)
  - **Authors**: Wilson Yan, Danijar Hafner, Stephen James, Pieter Abbeel
  - **Citations**: ICML 2023
  - **Influence**: 90/100

- **Paper**: World Model on Million-Length Video and Language with Blockwise Ringattention
  - **Authors**: Hao Liu, Wilson Yan, Pieter Abbeel
  - **Citations**: 289
  - **Influence**: 88/100
